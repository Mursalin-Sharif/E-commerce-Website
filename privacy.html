Privacy Policy

What we store

This demo site stores language preference, cart items, and optional login name/email in your browser’s localStorage only.

What we do not collect

There is no backend server. Contact form submissions are not sent to a remote API. Payment details are never requested.

Third-party links

The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with a configurable phone number. Use of WhatsApp is governed by Meta’s policies.

Your control

Clear site data in your browser to remove cart, language, and demo session information at any time.
